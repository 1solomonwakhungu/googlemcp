# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project
space, and when you represent the project elsewhere.

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or personal
  attacks.
- Do not publish other people's private information, including email
  addresses and account details, without their permission.
- Accept constructive feedback and give it the same way.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. For anything you
do not want to discuss in public, use GitHub's private reporting: open the
repository's **Security** tab, click **Report a vulnerability**, and start
the title with "Conduct report". For less sensitive matters, open an issue.

Maintainers will review every report, keep the reporter's identity private,
and respond with the enforcement steps described in the Contributor
Covenant's
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban.
